Strategies

A strategy places an order for you when a price crosses a level, with nobody confirming it. A BUY watches the ask and fires when it falls to your level; a SELL watches the bid and fires when it rises to it. A new strategy starts disabled, and disabling one stops it at once and cancels its working orders. Try a dry run first: it logs what it would do and places nothing.
